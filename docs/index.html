Turning hydrological data into emotions

Noéa reads a record like an ECG, for rhythm, memory and shock

Noéa has two complementary organs. Climate Pulse reads long records, from decades to two centuries of rainfall and river flow, for the rhythm and memory of a climate. The Human-Water Interface (HWI) works at the scale of one river and the people beside it, with short, fast records from a few nearby sensors.
